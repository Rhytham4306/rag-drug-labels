METFORMIN HYDROCHLORIDE TABLETS

DESCRIPTION
Metformin hydrochloride is an oral antihyperglycemic drug used in the
management of type 2 diabetes mellitus. It is available as 500 mg, 850 mg,
and 1000 mg tablets.

INDICATIONS AND USAGE
Metformin is indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve
glycemic control in adults and children 10 years of age and older with
type 2 diabetes mellitus.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION
Adults: the usual starting dose is 500 mg twice daily or 850 mg once daily,
given with meals. Dosage may be increased gradually in increments of 500
mg weekly, up to a maximum of 2550 mg per day in divided doses.

CONTRAINDICATIONS
Metformin is contraindicated in patients with severe renal impairment
(eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m2), and in patients with known hypersensitivity
to metformin.

WARNINGS
Lactic acidosis is a rare but serious metabolic complication that can occur
due to metformin accumulation. The risk increases with conditions such as
renal impairment, sepsis, dehydration, and excessive alcohol intake.
Metformin should be temporarily discontinued prior to procedures involving
iodinated contrast materials.

ADVERSE REACTIONS
The most common adverse reactions are gastrointestinal: diarrhea, nausea,
vomiting, flatulence, and abdominal discomfort. These are usually transient
and occur during initiation of therapy.

DRUG INTERACTIONS
Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors, alcohol, and cationic drugs eliminated by
renal tubular secretion may increase metformin levels. Concurrent use with
insulin or sulfonylureas may increase the risk of hypoglycemia.

USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
Renal impairment: assess renal function before starting therapy and at
least annually thereafter. Pediatric use: safety and effectiveness have
been established in pediatric patients 10 to 16 years of age.

OVERDOSAGE
Overdose of metformin, or concurrent risk factors, has been associated
with lactic acidosis, which is a medical emergency requiring hospital
treatment. Hemodialysis may be used to remove metformin.

HOW SUPPLIED
Tablets are supplied in bottles of 100 and 500 count.
