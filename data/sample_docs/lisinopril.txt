LISINOPRIL TABLETS

DESCRIPTION
Lisinopril is an angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitor used to
treat hypertension and heart failure. Available in 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg,
20 mg, and 40 mg tablets.

INDICATIONS AND USAGE
Lisinopril is indicated for the treatment of hypertension, to reduce risk
of stroke and cardiovascular events, for the treatment of heart failure as
an adjunct therapy, and for improving survival following acute myocardial
infarction.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION
Hypertension, adults: initial dose 10 mg once daily. Dosage should be
adjusted according to blood pressure response, typically in the range of
20-40 mg per day as a single dose. Heart failure: initial dose 5 mg once
daily, titrated to a target dose of 20-40 mg once daily as tolerated.

CONTRAINDICATIONS
Lisinopril is contraindicated in patients with a history of angioedema
related to previous ACE inhibitor treatment, and should not be
co-administered with aliskiren in patients with diabetes.

WARNINGS
ACE inhibitors can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman;
discontinue as soon as pregnancy is detected. Angioedema of the face, lips,
tongue, and/or larynx has been reported and may occur at any time during
treatment. Symptomatic hypotension may occur, especially in
volume-depleted patients.

ADVERSE REACTIONS
The most commonly reported adverse reactions are dizziness, headache,
cough, and hyperkalemia. Hypotension may occur, particularly after the
first dose in patients who are volume- or salt-depleted.

DRUG INTERACTIONS
Concomitant use with potassium-sparing diuretics, potassium supplements, or
salt substitutes containing potassium may lead to hyperkalemia. NSAIDs may
reduce the antihypertensive effect of lisinopril and increase the risk of
renal impairment when used together.

USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
Pregnancy: Category D — discontinue lisinopril as soon as possible once
pregnancy is detected. Renal impairment: dosage adjustment is required in
patients with reduced creatinine clearance.

OVERDOSAGE
The most likely manifestation of overdosage would be hypotension.
Treatment is generally supportive, including intravenous fluids for
hypotension.

HOW SUPPLIED
Tablets are supplied in bottles of 30, 90, and 1000 count.
